Withdrawal strategy

Otium first uses up to £12,000 a year from the existing crystallised pensions, then up to £6,000 a year from the Unit Trust, then cash/fixed-term savings and ISAs. Once those sources are insufficient it draws from the non-crystallised pension using £100,000 UFPLS tranches: 25% is treated as tax-free and 75% as taxable. The model prefers the first £12,000 of taxable UFPLS withdrawals each year, then tax-free cash, and uses further taxable pension withdrawals if required to meet spending.

Gifting

Enter a one-off gift, the age at which it is made, and the investment pot it comes from. The gift is deducted from that pot before that year's spending and is included in the forecast and depletion age.
